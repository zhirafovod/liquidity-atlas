Russia

IndicatorTrendsNotes
M2 GrowthOften 10-30%+ historically; ~12% recent (M2 ~130-140T RUB)High growth linked to inflation. Fiscal dominance in recent years (war spending).
InflationHigh in 90s/2000s, targeted 4%; recent 8-12%+Spikes with ruble depreciation, sanctions, money growth. Real estate as main sink.
Real EstateMassive nominal rises (secondary ~20-26x since late 90s)Subsidized mortgages (preferential rates) created bubble; ended recently. Money hits RE/goods fast due to shallow financial markets.

As Ilya noted: Without deep stock/debt markets, excess money flows to real assets (RE, construction) → rapid consumer inflation. Illegal migration/social subsidies increase demand. Inflation acts as tax on ruble holders.

Common themes: Money growth relative to productivity determines inflation or asset price rises depending on financial market depth and velocity. Immigration ↑ demand/consumption. Subsidies amplify sinks (RE). Inflation = indirect tax; direct taxes drain liquidity. Productivity (AI) increases Q, allowing higher M without P rise. Stock markets deeper in US/EU/CN than Russia historically.
